# Mentor Connect — One-Page Product Design Rationale

**Product**: Mentor Connect  
**Institution**: Mesa School of Business  
**Target User**: Mesa Fellows (Business & Technology Transitioners)  

---

## 1. The Core Problem in Executive Mentorship

In conventional mentorship platforms, students browse static directory cards and book sessions based on brand names or superficial titles. This leads to three severe failure modes:
1. **Unprepared Sessions**: Students arrive with vague questions like "Can you review my resume?" wasting high-value practitioner time.
2. **Mismatched Expertise**: A student struggling with n8n webhook idempotency books a senior VC partner whose expertise is macro fund thesis, leading to mutual frustration.
3. **Zero Follow-Through**: Advice given on a call evaporates without accountability, leaving students stuck at the same career blocker weeks later.

---

## 2. Our Product Solutions & UX Decisions

### Why Direct Booking Is Locked Behind AI Diagnosis
Instead of allowing students to impulsively book slots from the directory, Mentor Connect requires an interactive diagnostic conversation with the **AI Career Advisor**.
- The AI diagnoses the student's immediate blocker, current skills, previous attempts, and desired takeaway.
- It verifies that the challenge aligns with a mentor's verified background from the 50 anonymised profiles.
- **Outcome**: When booking is unlocked, the student understands *why* that mentor is appropriate, and a tailored **Pre-Call Preparation Brief** is automatically synthesized.

### Transparent Explainability Over Black-Box Percentages
Generic "99% match" badges breed skepticism. Mentor Connect provides **explainable matching**:
- A transparent heuristic score reflecting domain, sector, and AI tool overlap.
- Exact citations of the mentor's verified dataset background.
- Specific topics the mentor can advise on versus potential uncertainties.
- Transparent contrast against other profiles.

### Evidence-Linked Post-Call Reports
Rather than generic AI summaries, the platform anchors each insight directly to a timestamped excerpt from the meeting transcript:
- Students can expand any insight to see the mentor's exact quote and timestamp.
- Action items are concrete tasks with defined deliverables and deadlines, persisted in the database and tied directly to the student's **Career Roadmap**.

---

## 3. Visual Aesthetic & Brand Positioning
- **Palette**: Warm neutrals (`#FAF8F5`, `#1C1917`), off-white cards, and restrained emerald (`#059669`) and terracotta accents reflect executive business education.
- **Tone**: Focused, academic, and professional — avoiding generic cartoonish AI gradients and cluttered dashboards.
